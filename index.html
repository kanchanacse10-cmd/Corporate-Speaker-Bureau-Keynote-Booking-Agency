Frequently Asked Questions

Essential guidance on speaker honorariums, contractual escrow, travel riders, and recording rights for event directors.

Speaker honorariums are determined by the speaker’s market tier, event format (in-person vs. virtual), presentation duration, audience size, and geographic travel requirements. Our quotes are transparent, direct-from-bureau, and itemized with verified fee parity and zero hidden agency markups.
When you submit a booking inquiry or shortlist, our dedicated booking managers hold a direct soft calendar option directly with the speaker or their personal management. Holds remain active for up to 7 business days during board review, and are formally locked once the booking agreement is signed.
For in-person engagements, standard riders include roundtrip business-class airfare, executive hotel accommodations, airport ground transfers, and a per-diem meal allowance. Travel logistics can be coordinated directly by your company or managed completely through our agency dispatch coordinators.
Typically, a fifty percent (50%) deposit is due upon contract execution to reserve the speaker's date on the master calendar. The remaining fifty percent (50%) balance is due thirty (30) days prior to the event date. All client funds are protected in audited escrow accounts with multi-currency wire support.
Recording and broadcasting rights vary by speaker. Internal company archival recording is pre-cleared across almost all standard bureau riders. If you plan to stream to external audiences, distribute across teams, or repurpose footage, these terms are pre-negotiated and documented in the contract addendum.
