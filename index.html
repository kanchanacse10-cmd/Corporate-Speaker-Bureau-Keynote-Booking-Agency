01
Pricing & Honoraria

How does SummitSpeak determine speaker fees?

Speaker honorariums are determined by the speaker’s market tier, event format (in-person vs. virtual), presentation duration, audience size, and geographic travel requirements. Our quotes are transparent and itemized without unexpected markups.
✦ Fee Transparency: Itemized quotes with direct fee parity and zero hidden markups
02
Calendar Reservation

How is speaker availability confirmed?

When you submit a booking request or shortlist, our dedicated booking managers hold a soft calendar option directly with the speaker or their personal management. A date is formally locked once the booking agreement is signed and the initial deposit is processed.
✦ Hold Protocol: Soft calendar options active for up to 7 business days during board review
03
Travel & Logistics

What travel and accommodation arrangements are required?

For in-person engagements, standard riders typically include roundtrip business-class airfare (or first-class for transcontinental flights), executive hotel accommodations, airport ground transfers, and a per-diem meal allowance. Travel logistics can be coordinated directly by your company or managed through our agency.
✦ Rider Management: Dedicated dispatch coordinator provided for all global itineraries
04
Contracts & Escrow

What are the deposit and payment terms?

Typically, a fifty percent (50%) deposit is due upon contract execution to reserve the speaker's date on the calendar. The remaining fifty percent (50%) balance is due thirty (30) days prior to the event date. Invoices and payment receipts are accessible in your client portal.
✦ Escrow Security: Audited escrow custody with multi-currency wire and invoice generation
05
Media & Recording

Can we record or livestream the keynote presentation?

Recording and broadcasting rights vary by speaker. Most speakers permit internal non-commercial recording for company archives. If you plan to stream to external audiences or commercialize the footage, this is specified and agreed upon in the contract rider.
✦ Broadcast Rights: Internal company archival use pre-cleared across all standard riders
