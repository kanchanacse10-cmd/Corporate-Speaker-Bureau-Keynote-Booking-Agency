Phase 02

Holds & Commercial Lock-In

Securing speaker calendar priority and negotiating all commercial parameters, rider specifications, and travel budgets.

03
Portal Booking

Submit a Formal Booking Request

Select your preferred candidate, verify keynote duration and logistics, and submit a formal request through our multi-step booking engine, immediately generating a unique tracking token.

Client Deliverables & Outputs
  • Dedicated Client Portal tracking dashboard activated
  • Real-time email and SMS milestone status tracking
  • Executive Account Director assignment within 60 minutes
Milestone: Reference # Generated Start Request →
04
Management Clearance

Confirm Availability & Commercial Terms

The bureau coordinates calendar holds directly with speaker personal management, locks proposed honorariums, confirms travel parameters, and reviews audiovisual specifications.

Client Deliverables & Outputs
  • 14-day exclusive calendar hold confirmed with speaker agency
  • Travel rider caps and standard hotel allowance agreed
  • Recording rights & intellectual property parameters clarified
Milestone: Date Locked In Guaranteed Hold
Phase 03

Governance, Contracts & Escrow

Bulletproof legal riders, transparent corporate billing, and segregated escrow banking to protect institutional clients.

05
Legal Architecture

Review the Engagement Contract

Authorized corporate legal parties review the tripartite booking agreement in the Client Portal, verifying rider terms, deposit timelines, force majeure, and cancellation covenants.

Client Deliverables & Outputs
  • Standardized, vetted Bureau Agreement (Enterprise compliant)
  • Explicit Force Majeure, replacement, and refund covenants
  • Secure e-Signature workflow via HelloSign / DocuSign API
Milestone: Contract Signed Legal Clearance
06
Financial Security

Complete Booking & Escrow Deposit

The booking status transitions to "Confirmed" once the agreement is executed and the initial 50% deposit is secured into our protected client escrow account via wire or corporate card.

Client Deliverables & Outputs
  • Instant VAT/Tax compliant corporate tax invoice issued
  • Segregated escrow holding protecting your corporate funds
  • Formal Booking Confirmation Certificate released for stakeholders
Milestone: Booking Legally Locked Confirmed

Production Alignment

Prepare for Flawless Event Execution

Institutional Standards

The SummitSpeak Bureau Assurance Model

Every booking is backed by three non-negotiable operational safeguards that eliminate cancellation vulnerabilities.

Financial Escrow & Assurance

Deposits are held in segregated, audited escrow accounts. Honorariums are released to talent only post-event upon verified presentation delivery.

Contractual Rigor & SLAs

Custom tripartite corporate riders containing enforceable replacement covenants, standard travel policy caps, and intellectual property authorizations.
